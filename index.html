Mission

Kids who ask how things work should get to find out.

Fighting for Engineering Youth exists so that a child's curiosity about how things work does not depend on what their school can afford or who their family happens to know. We bring materials, working engineers and hands-on projects to young people who want to build.

Real tools
Kits, parts and workspace that kids can use and keep using, not demos behind glass.
Working mentors
Engineers, college students and teachers who judge, advise and answer honestly, including when the answer is "I got that wrong too."
Long runs
Projects that break and get fixed across several workshops. That is where most of the learning happens.

Bridge Design Competition

  • The competition

    A series of free workshops where participants design and build a bridge from simple materials, test it under load, and improve it between sessions. The series ends with a final competition where every finished bridge is tested.

    CostFree
    WhenOctober 2026, dates being confirmed
    WhereLocal libraries, being confirmed

  • Classroom kits

    Our next goal is to raise money for bridge and building kits for science programs in local schools that have little equipment, and to run workshops there.

    StatusPlanning
    ForLocal school science programs

How it works

  1. Design

    Sketch a bridge and plan how to use the materials.

  2. Build

    Assemble it from simple materials.

  3. Test

    Add weight until the bridge fails.

  4. Improve

    Study the failure and redesign between workshops.

  5. Compete

    Every finished bridge is tested at the final.
